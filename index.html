Got questions?

Everything you need to know.

A few quick answers to help you understand how Finora works.

Finora is an AI-powered business spend management platform designed to help businesses track expenses, organize transactions and understand their financial activity.

Finora is designed to bring supported business financial data into one place, helping organize and analyze transactions through automated workflows.

AI can help categorize transactions, identify spending patterns, highlight unusual activity and generate useful financial insights.

Finora is designed to provide departmental spending visibility, helping businesses understand how budgets are being utilized across teams.

Finora can highlight unusual spending patterns and recurring costs that may deserve a closer review. Final decisions remain with the business.

Finora's intended approach is to prioritize data protection, access controls and responsible handling of financial information. Specific security certifications and safeguards should only be advertised once implemented and verified.
